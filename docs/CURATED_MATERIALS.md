# Curated Materials

## HDPE – High-Density Polyethylene
- Source: EPA Plastic Identification Guide
- URL: https://www.epa.gov/recycling-materials/plastic-identification-codes
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: Low toxicity, ensure dust control during shredding

## PET – Polyethylene Terephthalate
- Source: EPA Plastic Identification Guide
- URL: https://www.epa.gov/recycling-materials/plastic-identification-codes
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: Prone to moisture degradation, dry before melting

## PP – Polypropylene
- Source: Plastics Europe – Polypropylene
- URL: https://plasticseurope.org/knowledge-hub/polypropylene/
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: Low toxicity, good fatigue resistance

## LDPE – Low-Density Polyethylene
- Source: EPA Plastic Identification Guide
- URL: https://www.epa.gov/recycling-materials/plastic-identification-codes
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: Low melt temp, monitor for scorching

## PS – Polystyrene
- Source: EPA Plastic Identification Guide
- URL: https://www.epa.gov/recycling-materials/plastic-identification-codes
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: Medium toxicity, avoid acetone exposure

## PVC – Polyvinyl Chloride
- Source: VinylPlus Health and Safety
- URL: https://www.vinylplus.eu/health-safety
- Review status: verified
- Safety notes: High toxicity, releases HCl gas when overheated or burned. Avoid without professional controls.
